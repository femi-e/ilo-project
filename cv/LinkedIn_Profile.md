### NHS Missed Appointments Analysis

*Tools: Python, Pandas, SQL, Tableau*
Analysed 600,000+ appointment records across 42 NHS organisations. Found that booking window is the strongest predictor of missed appointments — same-day bookings have a 2% DNA rate, appointments booked 28+ days out hit 10.95%. Recommended text reminders. Estimated £67M in annual savings.
