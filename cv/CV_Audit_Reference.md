# CV Audit Reference — Career Change to Data Analyst

Based on research from Rejectless, ResumeAdapter, TailorCV, CV4Me, SkillsetMaster, and MentorCruise (2026).
Applied writing style: active voice, short sentences, specific details, no em dashes.

---

## Research Findings

### 1. CV Length: One Page

For entry-level candidates with 0-3 years of experience, a one-page CV is the standard across every major source in 2026. Removing Soho House is the right call. It saves space and removes the least relevant role.

### 2. Domain-Specific Portfolio Projects

Every source agrees: generic datasets (Titanic, bike-sharing, housing) prove you can follow a tutorial. Domain-native projects prove you understand the business. For a hospitality-to-analytics transition, a hospitality project is the single highest-ROI addition you can make.

Examples that exist in the real world:

- Hotel booking cancellation and revenue loss analysis
- Restaurant POS data analysis (order patterns, peak times, menu performance)
- Hotel revenue management (ADR, RevPAR, occupancy trends)
- Restaurant delivery analytics (customer retention, churn, discount effectiveness)

### 3. CV Structure for Career Changers

The order matters: Summary > Skills > **Projects** > Experience > Education.

Projects come before experience because they prove you can do the work. Experience proves you have had jobs. For a career changer, the evidence matters more than the history.

### 4. ATS Keywords Every Data Analyst CV Needs

| Category | Keywords |
| ---------- | ---------- |
| Core tools | SQL, Python, Tableau, Excel |
| Analysis | Data cleaning, statistical testing, regression, segmentation |
| Domain | Hospitality operations, inventory analytics, POS data |
| Output | Dashboards, visualisations, actionable recommendations |

Use keywords naturally in context. Never list them without a result attached.

## Why a Hospitality Project Would Help

Every source makes the same point: domain-native projects land interviews. A hiring manager at a restaurant group or hotel chain will read your hospitality project because you understand their world. They will skim a generic Kaggle project.

The SkillsetMaster guide says: "Your industry knowledge is the hardest thing to acquire. Analytics skills can be learned in months. Domain expertise takes years. Together, they make you uniquely valuable."

A hospitality project also solves a structural problem: your current portfolio has NHS (healthcare), 2Market (retail), and Turtle Games (gaming). None of them connect to your background. A hospitality project completes the story: you came from hospitality, you understand hospitality data, and you can analyse it.

### Suggested Project Ideas

1. **Hotel Booking Cancellations** — Use the Hotel Booking Demand dataset (Kaggle) to analyse cancellation patterns, revenue loss, and recommend a dynamic cancellation policy. Tools: Python, Pandas, SQL, Tableau.

2. **Restaurant Operations Analysis** — Use POS-simulated data to analyse peak hours, average order value by day, menu item performance, and staff scheduling efficiency. Tools: Excel, SQL, Tableau.

3. **Bar Inventory Optimisation** — Use inventory and sales data to identify wastage patterns, slow-moving items, and optimal reorder points. Directly extends your Roka experience. Tools: Python, Excel, Tableau.

## Key Principles Applied in the Rewrite

- One page. Every bullet earns its place.
- Projects section leads and carries the weight.
- Soho House removed. Two hospitality roles retained.
- No em dashes in body text.
- Every bullet names the tool, the data size or scope, and the result.
- Active voice throughout. No hedging.
- Professional but warm tone.
