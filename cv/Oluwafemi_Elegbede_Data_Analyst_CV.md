## Projects

### NHS Missed Appointments Analysis

*Python, Pandas, SQL, Tableau, SciPy* — LSE Data Analytics Programme | 2026

- Worked through 600,000+ appointment records across 42 NHS ICBs over 30 months to find what drives missed appointments.
- Booking window was the strongest predictor by far. DNA rates jump from 2% for same-day bookings to 10.95% for appointments booked 28+ days out. That's a 5.6x increase, confirmed with chi-squared tests.
- Telephone appointments outperformed face-to-face consistently (2.11% DNA vs 6.01%). GP staffing levels had no meaningful link to DNA rates.
- Recommended text message reminders for long-window bookings. Estimated £67M in annual savings if rolled out across the NHS.

### 2Market Customer Segmentation

*PostgreSQL, Excel (Power Query), Tableau, CRISP-DM* — LSE Data Analytics Programme | 2026

- Built the full pipeline: exploratory analysis in Excel and Power Query, a SQL data mart for structured querying, and Tableau dashboards to present findings.
- Created 8 customer profiles from demographic data and spotted a clear Pareto effect — 4 segments covering 21.7% of the market drive 45% of revenue.
- Found that 58-63% of transactions still happen in-store even when customers research online first. Recommended a QR-coupon strategy to turn digital browsing into footfall.

### Turtle Games Loyalty Prediction

*Python (Scikit-learn), R (tidyverse, ggplot2)* — LSE Data Analytics Programme | 2026

- Built regression and decision tree models to predict customer loyalty from spending behaviour, demographics, and engagement data.
- Ran K-means clustering (k=5, silhouette score 0.582) to group customers by income and spending. Found that loyalty drivers are completely different across segments. Income explains 65% of loyalty variance for high-value customers but only 8.9% for growth segments.
- One-size-fits-all loyalty strategies don't work. Recommendations need to match what drives each segment.
