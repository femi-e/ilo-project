## Projects

### NHS Missed Appointments Analysis

*Python, Pandas, SQL, Tableau, SciPy* | LSE Data Analytics Programme | 2026

- Analysed 600,000+ appointment records across 42 NHS ICBs over 30 months to identify what drives missed appointments.
- Found that booking window is the strongest predictor. DNA rates jump from 2% for same-day bookings to 10.95% for appointments booked 28+ days out, a 5.6x increase confirmed with chi-squared tests.
- Telephone appointments outperformed face-to-face (2.11% DNA vs 6.01%). GP staffing levels showed no meaningful link.
- Recommended text message reminders for long-window bookings. Estimated GBP 67 million in annual savings if adopted across the NHS.

### 2Market Customer Segmentation

*PostgreSQL, Excel (Power Query), Tableau, CRISP-DM* | LSE Data Analytics Programme | 2026

- Built the full pipeline: exploratory analysis in Excel, a SQL data mart for structured querying, and Tableau dashboards for presentation.
- Created 8 customer profiles and spotted a clear Pareto effect. Four segments covering 21.7% of the market drive 45% of revenue.
- Found that 58-63% of transactions still happen in-store even when customers research online first. Recommended a QR-coupon strategy to convert digital browsing into footfall.

### Turtle Games Loyalty Prediction

*Python (Scikit-learn), R (tidyverse, ggplot2)* | LSE Data Analytics Programme | 2026

- Built regression and decision tree models to predict customer loyalty from spending behaviour, demographics, and engagement data.
- Ran K-means clustering (k=5, silhouette score 0.582) to group customers by income and spending. Found that loyalty drivers differ completely across segments.
- Income explains 65% of loyalty variance for high-value customers but only 8.9% for growth segments. Recommended segment-specific retention strategies.
